If macOS blocks the app

After install, if macOS says the app cannot be opened: click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, then click Open Anyway next to the DJ Genie message. Or Control-click the app → Open → Open.

Catalina includes Track Rescue search; automatic restore stays disabled on Catalina.
